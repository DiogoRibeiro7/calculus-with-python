# Instructor rubric

## Exercise notebooks

| Criterion | Points | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All TODOs addressed | 10 | Each exercise notebook contains six tasks; reward completion with explanations. |
| Figures saved and referenced | 5 | Verify `assets/figures/` contains the promised outputs. |
| Reflection/markdown answers | 5 | Students should note what they learned or struggled with. |

## Projects / mini-lesson deliverables

- **Mini-projects**: Evaluate whether plots convey correct extremum locations, integration errors, and whether the reflection/exercises are answered.
- **Common mistakes notebook**: Points for documenting the failure, running the fixed version, and interpreting the saved plots.

## Narrative & documentation

- Encourage references to `docs/concepts.md`, `docs/faq.md`, and `docs/gallery.md` when students ask conceptual questions.
